Textile Lineage

Honoring the Intimate Science of Everyday Footwear

Founded at 181 Mercer Street in Manhattan, POLYAMIDE CREW reclaims legwear from thoughtless disposable commodities into enduring ergonomic architecture.

By uniting classical hand-knitting sensibilities with state-of-the-art circular Italian knitting cylinders, our craftspeople achieve stitch density parameters that commercial mills cannot match.

Atelier Standards

The Three Pillars of Legwear Longevity

200-Needle Cylinder Gauge

Ultra-dense circular knitting locks fibers together, creating a silky handfeel that maintains structural integrity across hundreds of wash cycles.

Reinforced Heel & Toe Armor

Double-twisted polyamide threads reinforce critical high-abrasion zones, multiplying wear life fourfold compared to standard department store socks.

Direct Cooperative Sourcing

We source long-staple Egyptian cotton and certified recycled polyamide directly from ethical spin houses, guaranteeing zero child labor and fair wages.
