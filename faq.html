Frequently Addressed Hosiery Questions

Pure cotton lacks tensile memory and stretches out after hours of walking. Micro-filament polyamide acts as an internal elastic armature, providing shape retention, rapid moisture dispersion, and quadruple the abrasion resistance of pure cotton.

Commercial socks join the toe closure with a high-speed overlock machine that leaves a bulky, raised ridge across the toes. Hand-linking connects stitch-to-stitch in a completely flat plane, completely preventing chafing and blisters inside snug shoes.

Depending on yarn procurement and custom dye matching, bespoke orders require between three and six weeks of dedicated cylinder setup, test looping, and hand steam blocking.

We recommend washing inside-out in cold water on a gentle cycle with mild detergent, followed by air-drying flat. Avoiding high-heat tumble dryers preserves the internal elastomer fibers indefinitely.

Every piece includes a comprehensive twenty-four-month warranty covering structural seam integrity, heel wear, and elastic recovery under normal metropolitan care.
