Architectural Fiber & Knitwork Principles

Pillar I

Micro-Filament Polyamide Core

High-tenacity nylon filaments wrapped in organic combed cotton deliver multi-directional tensile resilience and complete anti-chafing blister defense.

Pillar II

Hand-Linked Seamless Toe

Every toe seam is closed needle-by-needle with manual loop linkage, eliminating ridge pressure and providing flawless comfort inside bench-made leather shoes.

Pillar III

Calibrated Ribbed Welt Tension

Engineered with zoned elastomer compression that stays up comfortably above the ankle throughout eighteen hours of metropolitan transit without constriction.
